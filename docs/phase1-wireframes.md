# Phase 1: grey wireframes

File: `wireframes/index.html` (open in a browser; greyscale at 375 px, no colour, no photos).

The client asked us to go ahead without stopping at the gates, so the recommended option of each pair was built. The other options are listed here so Vedant can still switch; each is a small change.

## Landing hero (screen 1)

| Option | What it is | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A, built** | Full-bleed 4:5 hand video, text and button below | Photo carries the look; text stays readable whatever the video; the poster is the LCP image. Closest to the reel she just tapped. |
| B | Text over the video | Most immersive, but contrast depends on each new video and the trust line gets squeezed. |
| C | Price and product grid first | Fastest to products, but drops the emotional hook the ad set up. |

Screens 2 (choose: moods, bundle offers, kit teaser) and 3 (proof: reels, 3 steps, one real review, one button) have one layout each.

## Product page buy box

All options follow the rules: no size pre-selected, "Size me later" offered, bundle choice above Add to bag, Buy now for one set only.

| Option | What it is | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A, built** | Stacked: size, then sets, then buttons | One clear path down the page. Buttons look inactive until a size (or Size me later) is picked; tapping anyway shows a polite note at the size step. |
| B | Sets first, size in a sheet | Pushes bundles harder, but hides the size choice, where most of the doubt sits. |
| C | Three small bundle tiles | Compact, but offer copy gets cramped at 375 px and reads as "sale". |

## Cart drawer

| Option | What it is | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A, built** | Gift bar on top, lines, nudge, add-ons, savings, one button | Order of attention: reward, what she's buying, the next set, then pay. Nothing floats over checkout. |
| B | Upsell card with bestsellers at the top | Stronger second-set push, but busier, and the bag slips below the fold. |
| C | Minimal | Calmest, but leaves out the gift bar and add-ons the brief needs. |

## Changes found while building

- **Sticky bar on phones:** on a 375 px phone the main Add to bag sits below the first screen. The sticky bar now shows whenever the main buttons are off screen, reading "Choose your size" until a size is picked. Tapping it scrolls to the size step.
